# Creator product identity
> updated 2026-09-27 · v0.2.0

## Audience and job
Primary: one Vietnamese movie-review creator producing an 8–12 minute original commentary video from footage they are authorized to use. Secondary: a small channel that needs multiple drafts and platform handoff; multi-user SaaS is not the current target.

## Positioning
Windows-local review desk that links each draft claim, narration cue and chosen film shot to its source timecode, then lets the creator inspect and edit a playable review before export. Proposed differentiator, **unverified** until creators compare their own workflows: from an owned movie file to a source-grounded review and editable timeline in one project without repeatedly searching the film in another editor.

## Product outcome
A creator can set a thesis and style, inspect factual/story evidence, approve the script, hear actual timed narration, correct scenes/captions, run technical and editorial QA, then export an MP4 and upload package. There is no claim that automated watermark masking grants reuse rights or that a technical pass guarantees YouTube monetization.

## Revenue and boundaries
Revenue path undecided: currently a personal/local creation tool, no subscription or price. Validate repeated solo use and measured hours saved on three different authorized sources before evaluating paid tiers. Local service uses FFmpeg/Whisper/FastEmbed; AGY/TTS calls may require network. Publish remains a human action.

## Proof to seek
Three authorized source films, creator-reviewed scene relevance and statement accuracy, a full play-through of every export, and clean-Windows cold start. Count manual fixes per 10-minute output and record whether another editor was needed; decide product scope from those results.
